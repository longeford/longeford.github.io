I am a PhD candidate at Université Paris Dauphine – PSL working on information asymmetries between banks and central banks, both in the design of banking regulation and in the conduct of banking supervision under the direction of [Sylvain Benoit](https://sites.google.com/site/sylvainbenoit87/) and [Sylvain Carré](https://sites.google.com/view/sylvain-carre/research). 

During the fall 2026 term, I am visiting the UCL School of Management at the invitation of Fred Malherbe.

Before my PhD, I worked as an analyst at the ECB and the Banque de France, conducting statistical analyses across EU banks to support the ECBs regulation of interest rate risk in the banking book.


Outside of research, I enjoy playing the guitar and the piano (in groups) and I spend my free time climbing and swimming (also in groups). A good recent novel that I read is Guillaume Chamanadjian's _Heureux comme jamais_.
