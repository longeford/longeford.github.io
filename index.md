I am a PhD candidate at Université Paris Dauphine – PSL working on financial intermediation under the direction of [Sylvain Benoit](https://sites.google.com/site/sylvainbenoit87/) and [Sylvain Carré](https://sites.google.com/view/sylvain-carre/research).

My research focuses on information asymmetries between banks and central banks, both in the design of banking regulation and in the conduct of banking supervision.

During the fall 2026 term, I am visiting the UCL School of Management at the invitation of Fred Malherbe.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing the guitar and the piano (in groups) and I spend my free time climbing and swimming (also in groups). A good recent novel that I read is Guillaume Chamanadjian's _Heureux comme jamais_.
